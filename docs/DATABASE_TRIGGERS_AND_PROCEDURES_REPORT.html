Academics & Grading Function fn_recalculate_student_result() marks
BEFORE INSERT/UPDATE		 Translates numerical marks into grade letters (O, A+, A, B+, B, C, F) and grade points (10 to 0). Computes semester SGPA: SUM(credits * grade_point) / SUM(credits) and cumulative CGPA.
Academics & Grading Trigger trg_marks_calc_results marks
BEFORE INSERT/UPDATE		 Fires when faculty grades marks. Automatically upserts results with Pass/Fail status and updates students.sgpa and students.cgpa.
Finance & Accounts Function fn_fee_payment_update() fee_payments
AFTER INSERT		 Sums all receipts for a student fee invoice; recalculates paid_amount and due_amount; updates invoice status to Paid or Partially Paid; updates students.fees_due.
Finance & Accounts Trigger trg_fee_payment_sync fee_payments
AFTER INSERT		 Fires when a fee payment receipt is generated. Instantly reflects zero balance on Student Fees and Finance Officer desk.

Library Management Trigger trg_library_fine_calc book_issues
BEFORE UPDATE		 Fires whenever a book issue record transitions to Returned or Overdue.
